Method

Owner cash = purchase minus residual + energy + maintenance + tyres + insurance + rego. GHG = petrol well-to-wheel + manufacture; BEV electricity blends 55 g/kWh for solar share with your grid factor for the rest. Battery build 55 kg CO2e/kWh LFP or 75 NMC. Deaths = health cost ÷ $5.3m VSL — a risk allocation, not a prediction.
