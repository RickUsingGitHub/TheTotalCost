Assumptions — and why they are there

This page is an argument with knobs, not a truth machine. Defaults are NSW / Australia 2026.

Why no hybrids

Hybrids sit in the middle and flatten the contrast. The question here is petrol ICE versus battery-electric. A RAV4 Hybrid beating a thirsty Santa Fe does not answer that. Custom mode is also petrol vs BEV.

Prices are RRP before on-roads

Stamp duty and delivery move with the state. Edit the prices if you have a drive-away quote.

Consumption

Claimed combined figures nudged toward real-world. Use the fields if you have a logbook.

Electricity mix

Solar is priced as foregone feed-in, not free. Off-peak is a NSW-style EV plan. DC is a public-network ballpark. Shares that do not add to 100 are scaled.

Grid carbon vs solar carbon

NSW NGER / EPA intensity is about 610–640 g/kWh in 2024–26. The solar share is given 55 g/kWh (panel life), not zero. Overnight coal charging is why a NSW BEV is not a Norwegian BEV.

Petrol well-to-wheel 2.85 kg/L

Tailpipe is about 2.31 kg/L. The extra is extraction, methane, shipping and refining — the pair to grid emissions for the BEV.

Battery manufacture

55 kg CO2e/kWh LFP, 75 NMC, plus 1.5 t extra glider and 8 t shared body. Mid literature. No pack swap in the base case.

Separate years, residual, tyres

A BEV may be kept longer or dumped earlier. Residuals have been kind to some EVs and cruel to others. BEVs are heavier and wear tyres faster (default 3.0 c vs 1.8 c / km). Split columns exist so those need not be identical.

Insurance and rego

Insurance as a percent of list price is crude. EVs sometimes cost more to cover. Rego is treated as almost the same.

Two carbon prices, never added

ACCU spot (~$38/t mid-2026) is what you can pay to cancel a tonne on paper. Social cost of carbon (default $80/t) is a damage estimate. Adding them double-counts.

Air pollution and “deaths”

ICE $0.035/km and BEV $0.012/km are order-of-magnitude allocations from Australian TRAP / NSW on-road PM work spread across light-vehicle kilometres. BEV is tyres, road wear and power-station pollution. Statistical deaths = that bill ÷ $5.3m VSL. Not a prediction that this car kills someone.

Still out

Crashes, congestion, public road-wear, mine biodiversity, oil spills, child labour, noise, FBT, subsidies, charger time, wallbox capex, finance interest.
